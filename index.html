About

Part engineer, part editor.

I studied Computer Science at Ashford State University, then spent my early career at product studios learning that the best code disappears — it just makes the right thing feel obvious. Since then I've worked with startups and design teams to build interfaces, APIs, and the small tools in between that make a product feel finished.

Outside of client work, I keep a home darkroom — the theme of this site is not an accident. I like processes with a beginning, a middle, and a print at the end of it, and I try to bring that same patience to software.
