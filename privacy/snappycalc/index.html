Privacy Policy - SnappyCalc

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short

SnappyCalc works without an account. We do not collect or sell your personal information. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which has its own data practices, described below.

What you enter

The numbers and dates you type into the calculators, such as loan amounts, salary, date of birth, height and weight, are processed on your device only. They are not sent to us or to any server, and they are not saved after you close the app.

What is stored on your device

Only your appearance choice (system, light or dark), so the app opens the way you left it.

Internet use

The Currency Converter downloads exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). That request does not include anything you typed. Like any internet request, it makes your device's IP address visible to that service, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Ads also use the internet (see below). The calculators themselves work offline.

Advertising

SnappyCalc shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and other device and usage information, and may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not receive this information, and nothing you type into the calculators is sent to Google.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads. Where available, you can change your choice later in Settings under Ad privacy settings.

On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads). To learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics and permissions

SnappyCalc does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own, and it does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. The ads software may add the Android advertising ID permission to the app.

Contacting support

If you email support@snappycalc.app, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply to you.

Children

SnappyCalc is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Your data and choices

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no account or personal data for us to access, correct or delete. For ad-related data held by Google, use the ad settings on your phone or the choices described under Advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app with a new date.
